## The Talk

Spoke about how VMware leveraged Gainsight capabilities to deliver measurable customer outcomes — the intersection of customer success tooling and enterprise SaaS transformation. The talk covered how we built the CS function at VMware and used data-driven engagement to drive adoption across desktop-in-the-cloud, SaaS productivity, and VMware Cloud on AWS.
