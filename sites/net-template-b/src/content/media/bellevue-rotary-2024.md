## Key Points

Customer success measures customer growth and expansion — and AI is fundamentally changing how that engagement methodology works. I spoke about how AI shifts the CS playbook from reactive support to predictive, data-driven customer motions that scale.

## Why It Matters

AI doesn't just change products — it changes the entire relationship between a company and its customers. Customer success teams that understand this will outperform those that treat AI as a feature bolted onto the same old approach.
