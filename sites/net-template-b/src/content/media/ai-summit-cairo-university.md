## The Talk

A panel and keynote conversation at the AI Summit hosted at Cairo University, alongside regional technology leaders and academics. The session focused on how AI is reshaping the customer success function across enterprise cloud, what hyperscaler GTM motions look like in emerging markets, and the role universities and public institutions can play in building the talent pipeline for an AI-native economy.

## Why It Matters

Egypt's tech sector has been compounding fast — the engineering depth, the bilingual workforce, and the cost-to-quality ratio make Cairo one of the more interesting AI-delivery hubs in MENA. Speaking at Cairo University, where some of that next generation of engineers and PhDs are being trained, was as much about listening as it was about presenting. The questions from the room were as good as any I get at industry conferences.
