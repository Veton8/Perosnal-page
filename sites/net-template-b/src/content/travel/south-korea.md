South Korea was about building the future at the intersection of hardware and cloud — and finding pockets of community along the way.

## The Work

The key stories here were around robotic innovation development with AWS and Samsung. We built a joint partnership to embed Amazon WorkSpaces into Samsung's new tablet lineup for medical doctors. The robots were equipped with vision sensors and ran on AWS infrastructure using Aurora and Bedrock. Hospitals were the proving ground — workflows where a one-second delay in retrieving a patient record actually matters.

## Why It Stands Out

South Korea moves fast. The pace of innovation there — the willingness to prototype and ship — matched the AWS culture perfectly. Decisions that would take a quarter elsewhere happened in a meeting. It was one of the most productive partnerships I've been part of.

## Seoul Central Mosque

A favorite stop on every Seoul trip is the Seoul Central Mosque in Itaewon — the white-stone building with twin minarets that sits on a hill overlooking the neighborhood. It's the oldest mosque in South Korea, opened in 1976, and it serves a small but tight Muslim community. Stairs up to the prayer hall, palm trees, the same call to prayer I grew up hearing in Cairo, half a world away.
