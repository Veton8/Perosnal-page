South Korea was about building the future at the intersection of hardware and cloud.

## The Work

The key stories here were around robotic innovation development with AWS and Samsung. We built a joint partnership to embed Amazon WorkSpaces into Samsung's new tablet lineup for medical doctors. The robots were equipped with vision sensors and ran on AWS infrastructure using Aurora and Bedrock.

## Why It Stands Out

South Korea moves fast. The pace of innovation there — the willingness to prototype and ship — matched the AWS culture perfectly. It was one of the most productive partnerships I've been part of.
