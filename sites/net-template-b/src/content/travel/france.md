My time in France started as an internship that became one of the defining experiences of my early career.

## The Work

I worked in Paris as an intern for CFP (Total), the large French petroleum company, as a developer. These were the early days of the invention of spreadsheets — all operating systems ran Unix. I became a certified trainer for Open Access across Total.

## The Adventure

On weekends, I traveled through Europe on Eurail tickets as a student. Slept in dorms and made friendships that lasted years. There's no better way to see a continent than broke and curious.
