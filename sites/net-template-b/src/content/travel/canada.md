Canada was my third international move with Microsoft: from Riyadh to Paris to Toronto.

## The Work

I ran Microsoft Consulting Services in Canada. Worked with key banks including BMO and TD, as well as Toronto Power. I had an amazing senior team that spanned all Canadian cities, so I traveled constantly between Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, and my favorite — Montreal.

## The Personal Side

This was also a family chapter. My daughter grew up in Toronto and attended her first pre-school while I went back to Jiu Jitsu. I also had the opportunity to reconnect with a long-lost cousin and his Filipino wife — we hadn't seen each other for over 20 years. Canada has a way of making the world feel smaller.
