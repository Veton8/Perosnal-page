## A Diplomat's Son in South America

My father served as Consul General in Rio de Janeiro and Consul in Brasilia as part of his long diplomatic career. He was also Ambassador of Egypt to Australia (1985–1990), Ambassador to Jordan (1980–1984), and held postings in Washington DC, Damascus, and Beirut. Post-retirement, he represented Egypt on international forums including nuclear non-proliferation and acted as a state representative in foreign affairs across the Middle East, Africa, and the Far East. He was educated in Egypt and Oxford, traveled the world extensively, and spoke multiple languages.

Growing up in Brazil as the child of a diplomat is where I first learned what it means to be a global citizen — to walk into a room where nobody looks like you, sounds like you, or eats like you, and to find a way to belong anyway. That instinct has shaped every team I've built since.
