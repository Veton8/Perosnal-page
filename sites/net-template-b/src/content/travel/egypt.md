Egypt is home. Born and raised in Cairo, I return for personal trips and family reconnection.

## Cairo

Cairo is where the story starts. Growing up here as the son of a diplomat taught me what it means to carry warmth and ambition in the same hand — hospitality isn't a policy in Egypt, it's the default setting.

## Alexandria

The highlight of my Alexandria visits has been the Great Library of Alexandria and the ancient Alexandria Museum. For foodies, this is an amazing city for grilled seafood — some of the best I've had anywhere in the world.
