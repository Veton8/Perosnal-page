**Cities:** Seattle, Bay Area, Atlanta, Falls Church (VA)

Home is Seattle, WA — arguably one of the most beautiful cities in the world. The weather can be gray, but we get all four seasons. We have the highest number of coffee shops per capita, an amazing community, and we're very tech-heavy: home base for Microsoft, Amazon, and major presence for Google, Meta, and Oracle. Seattle is also the capital for Boeing, so there's significant industry around aerospace.

When my wife and I moved here in 2006, we knew this would be home. Two of our three girls were born here, and we have a great community with hundreds of friends.

Before Seattle: the Bay Area, Atlanta, and Falls Church, Virginia — each chapter of the American career left its mark, but Seattle is where we put down roots.
