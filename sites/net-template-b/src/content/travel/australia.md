Australia is where it all began. After university in Canberra, I stayed and launched my technology career.

## HiSoft, Canberra

I worked at HiSoft — a large Australian systems integrator that took on Federal Government contracts. I was a senior solutions architect engaged in all pre-sales and post-sales work. Certified in Microsoft, Netware, and SCO Linux.

I started as a support engineer and QA lead, then moved into development. Part-time game hacker on the side.

## The Foundation

Canberra in the early 1990s was an unlikely launchpad for a global tech career, but it gave me the fundamentals — how to work with large enterprise customers, how to architect solutions, and how to hustle when you're the new engineer who doesn't know anyone.
