I've been to Japan over ten times — Tokyo, Okinawa, and Kyoto — mostly on business and twice for pleasure.

## The Work

The highlights were building teams and coaching customers — NTT, Toyota, and various Japanese banks. At [VMware](/experience/vmware-customer-success/), I built up partner capacity through Hitachi. At [Microsoft](/experience/microsoft-director-roles/), I ran field enablement and sales excellence for the Japan subsidiary — a business generating over $1B — and worked with top customers and Microsoft Consulting to deploy datacenter solutions.

## The Experience

Japan teaches you patience and precision. Every business interaction has a structure and a rhythm. Once you learn to respect that rhythm, doors open that you didn't know existed.
