I was moved from the US (Charlotte, NC) to Riyadh to build and run Microsoft Consulting in the Middle East and Africa.

## The Scope

I had teams spanning Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, and South Africa. Key customers included STC, Aramco, and Citibank. I supported partner alliances as we scaled the business across the region.

## What It Taught Me

Running a consulting business across the Middle East and Africa from Riyadh taught me how to lead across cultures at the deepest level — different regulatory environments, different relationship protocols, different definitions of urgency. The Arabic language and my Egyptian background opened doors, but earning trust still required showing up and delivering, every time.
