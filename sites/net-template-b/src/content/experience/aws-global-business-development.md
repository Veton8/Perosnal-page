## The Business Problem

AWS was incubating new end-user computing products — Amazon WorkSpaces, WorkDocs, WorkMail, and Windows Application Manager. These needed to go from zero to real customer adoption and revenue.

## What We Did

Led engineering and go-to-market for the entire product suite. Worked with customers on use case identification, proof of value, and ROI assessments. Ran all field and technical sales enablement.

## The Outcome

Grew the business and landed the first 200 customers. Created a new revenue stream of $200M. Landed Johnson & Johnson as the first marquee lighthouse customer — their CIO spoke alongside Andy Jassy at AWS re:Invent.
