## The Business Problem

Intel needed to break into a new $1B+ market and make AI more accessible by providing an alternative hardware/software combination to NVIDIA. The challenge was building a credible SaaS go-to-market for a company historically rooted in silicon.

## What We Did

Co-built Intel Tiber SaaS services, including Intel Tiber Cloud — an AI-based training and inference platform. Created the roadmap for Intel Cloud and Workload Optimization Services. Built and led a 200+ person organization of architects and customer success managers.

## The Outcome

Created Intel's first-ever $200M ARR revenue stream from SaaS services. Landed Seekr.com and Inflection.AI as customers. Established the customer success playbook for Intel's next-generation cloud-SaaS business.
