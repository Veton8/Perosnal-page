## The Scope

Over twelve years at Microsoft, I held leadership roles in Business Management (Online Services), Business Architecture (Windows Devices & Applications), and Field Enablement — with assignments that took me to Russia, Japan, Saudi Arabia, and Canada.

## What We Did

Ran global solutions specialist teams across multiple product lines. Built solutions and repeatable business architecture across the Microsoft family of products — both cloud and on-premises. Ran all POCs for customers and oversaw key enterprise and lighthouse account deals. Managed all executive briefings and customer updates.

In [Japan](/travel/japan/), I ran all sales excellence for the Microsoft Japan subsidiary — a business generating over $1B — and worked with top customers and Microsoft Consulting to deploy datacenter solutions.

In [Saudi Arabia](/travel/saudi-arabia/), I built and ran Microsoft Consulting in the Middle East and Africa, with teams spanning Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, and South Africa. Key customers included STC, Aramco, and Citibank.

In [Canada](/travel/canada/), I ran Microsoft Consulting Services, working with major banks including BMO and TD, as well as Toronto Power. Led teams across Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, and Montreal.

## The Outcome

Built various incubation businesses around Windows — security and management, devices, and productivity workflow applications. Led enterprise go-to-market strategy for Windows 8 tablets.
