## The Business Problem

VMware was incubating new technology — desktop-in-the-cloud, SaaS productivity, and VMware Cloud on AWS. Customer adoption of these new product lines needed a dedicated success organization and an alliance management relationship with AWS.

## What We Did

Built VMware's EUC SaaS capabilities and created and ran VMware Customer Success from scratch. Led the customer success transformation across the entire product portfolio. Increased NPS scores and adoption of strategic technology including vSphere and NSX.

## The Outcome

Onboarded 200+ new customers on VMware Cloud on AWS (a brand-new product line). Resolved 200+ customer escalations. Increased NPS by 3+ points. Featured keynote speaker at Gainsight Pulse 2018 on driving SaaS growth through customer success.
