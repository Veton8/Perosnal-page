## Starting in Australia

My technology career started at HiSoft in Canberra, Australia — a large Australian systems integrator that took on Federal Government contracts. I was a senior solutions architect engaged in all pre-sales and post-sales work. Certified in Microsoft, Netware, and SCO Linux. I started as a support engineer and QA lead, then moved into development. Part-time game hacker on the side.

## The Move to America

After [Australia](/travel/australia/), I moved to the United States and spent a decade in operating and technology roles across enterprise software and systems — at Hewlett-Packard, XcelleNet (which became Sterling Commerce), Vanstar, and Georgia-Pacific.
