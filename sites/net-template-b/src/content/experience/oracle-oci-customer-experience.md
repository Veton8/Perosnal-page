## The Business Problem

Oracle was a late comer to cloud. We had to play catch-up to AWS and Azure — and we did it by working closely with customers and migrating Oracle applications to cloud.

## What We Did

Built and ran Oracle Cloud Customer Success, which included both pre-sales and post-sales delivery capability for OCI's top 200 customers. Led workload migration from on-premises to cloud, application optimization, and critical situational support.

## The Outcome

Grew the business through customer expansion from approximately $200M ARR to over $1.25B ARR. Built out the global team and landed the first two Oracle Dedicated Region deployments.
