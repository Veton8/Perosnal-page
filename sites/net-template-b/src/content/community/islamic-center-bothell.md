## The Series

I teach a seerah class on Sundays at the Islamic Center of Bothell. The series has been running for over two years — a weekly exploration of the Prophet's life and its lessons for how we live today.

## Why It Matters

The Weekly Knowledge Quest isn't a lecture — it's a running conversation. The same faces show up every week, the questions get deeper, and the community gets stronger. It's the most grounding thing I do all week.
