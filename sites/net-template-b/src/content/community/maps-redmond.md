## How I Got Involved

I served on the Board of Directors and then the Board of Trustees at MAPS. Now I contribute regularly through lectures, khutbas, and the Friday lessons.

## What I Teach

I lead the Muslim Experience Halaqa — a recurring educational discussion that explores what it means to live as a Muslim in the Pacific Northwest, balancing faith, career, community, and civic engagement.
